# math_scene_art

Target: Blender only (`_art`), delivered as renders in `renders/`.

## Brief

Build 3D shapes from mathematics (algebraic surfaces mostly), then turn them
into metal structures in the manner of Gustave Eiffel's riveted lattice iron,
or into iron-and-glass greenhouses in the Art Nouveau style.

Main file: `math_scene.blend`.

## Pipeline (draft, to confirm)

1. Base surface: parametric or implicit equation, generated by a Python script
   in `scripts/` (implicit surfaces meshed with Blender 5 grid nodes or
   marching cubes).
2. Member layout: curves on the surface (isoparametric lines, planar
   sections, or straight rulings), built in Geometry Nodes so it stays live.
3. Members: lattice girders, angle irons, I-sections swept along the curves;
   rivets and gussets as instances.
4. Glazing and ornament for the greenhouse variants.
5. Scene: ground, lighting, camera; Cycles for finals.

## Decisions

- Surfaces: all families. Ruled quadrics (hyperboloids, hyperbolic
  paraboloids), classic algebraic surfaces (Clebsch, Kummer, Cayley, Roman,
  Barth), Dupin cyclides and tori, and non-algebraic ones (catenoids, minimal
  surfaces, the Eiffel Tower profile).
- Two series on one shared pipeline:
  - Engineering: bare riveted lattice iron after Eiffel and Shukhov.
  - Greenhouses: glazed Art Nouveau iron and glass after Laeken and Auteuil.
- Real buildings at real size (10-60 m) in a setting with ground,
  surroundings, people for scale and sky.
- Output: stills only, no animation. Two looks:
  - photoreal (Cycles, HDRI daylight, painted or weathered iron, real glass);
  - period (sepia albumen print, or engraving/blueprint with Freestyle lines).
- Render format: 3:2 landscape, 3000x2000.
- Iron finish (photoreal): pristine, freshly painted as on opening day.
- First piece: Shukhov hyperboloid tower. It builds the lattice, rivet and
  material pipeline the other pieces reuse.
- Light (Shukhov tower): dusk. Poly Haven "Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky)", sun about
  2 degrees up and set just inside the left of the frame (`scripts/sky.py`),
  exposure -0.9 with AgX Medium High Contrast, mist from 15 m to 335 m
  (`scripts/render_setup.py`).
- Render device: GPU, Cycles with OptiX on the RTX 3070 Ti (CPU disabled in
  Preferences, scene device set to GPU). Check this before any long render.

## Second piece: cyclide palm house

- Art Nouveau glasshouse in the same park, at (-110, -130), with its own hero
  camera (`CAM_PalmHouse`, set in `scripts/shots.py`); midday light (below).
- Form: crescent of two tapering vaults cut from one Dupin cyclide band
  (a 18, c 3, d 4.5), round a glazed drum (r 9 m, 7.5 m) under a spindle-torus
  dome (9 m). Ribs and purlins are lines of curvature (circles), panes are
  planar. Base surface by numpy (`scripts/palm_house.py`), ironwork in
  Geometry Nodes on top.
- Steps: 1 massing, 2 ironwork and glass, 3 plinth, entrances and wing
  gables, 4 Art Nouveau ornament, 5 palms inside (all done), 6 site, camera,
  figures, light, renders (site, figures and light done; final render to do).
- Grounds (`scripts/palm_house_site.py`): gravel apron along the plinth, a
  terrace linking the three doors, a round bed on the axis inside a gravel
  ring, two beds following the inner curve of the wings, and a path curving
  from the ring under the camera to the tower path. Drawn as signed distance
  fields in `textures/palm_house_site_mask.exr`, which the ground material,
  the lawn scatter and the bedding all sample. Carpet bedding (white alyssum
  edging, red geraniums, yellow calceolaria and a tubbed fan palm in the round
  bed) in `scripts/palm_house_bedding.py`; a grove behind the house reuses
  the tree belt scatter; five visitors in `scripts/palm_house_figures.py`.
- Light (palm house): midday. Poly Haven "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure
  Sky)", sun 48 degrees up at azimuth 40, behind the camera's left shoulder
  (render v02 of the three tried), exposure -0.4 with AgX Medium High
  Contrast, lighter mist (strength 0.35).
- Shots: `scripts/shots.py` sets camera, sky preset (`sky.py`), grade
  (`render_setup.py`) and which lawn patch renders, per piece:
  `use_shot("palm_house")` or `use_shot("shukhov")`, then `render_version()`.
- Park overview (`use_shot("park")`, `CAM_Park`): both pieces from a rise 12 m
  up, west-northwest of the palm house, 24 mm. Midday Kloofendal with the sun
  turned to azimuth 130 for this view, thinner and deeper haze (grade
  `midday_wide`). `Tree_Park_Framing` (renders only in this shot) frames the
  left edge at hand-placed spots and closes the horizon with groves from 250 m
  out. Final: `renders/park_overview_photo_v01.png`.
